# Source credits and permission to cache

This pack is derived from public-domain raw vector data produced/distributed by
USGS The National Map. It contains no OSM/Google/rendered map tiles and no borrowed
commercial cartography. Offline storage and derived rendering are permitted by the
USGS public-domain policy. Credit: USGS TNM; U.S. Census Bureau TIGER/Line; U.S. Forest
Service; FAA-origin runway data distributed through USGS.

- https://www.usgs.gov/information-policies-and-instructions/copyrights-and-credits
- https://www.usgs.gov/faqs/are-usgs-reportspublications-copyrighted
- https://carto.nationalmap.gov/arcgis/rest/services/transportation/MapServer?f=pjson
- https://hydro.nationalmap.gov/arcgis/rest/services/nhd/MapServer?f=pjson

Both service descriptions explicitly identify their downloadable vector datasets as
public domain. Exact query URLs, selected object IDs and source checksums are in
source-manifest.json. Raw source files remain in the build workspace. Retrieved 2026-10-01; service
metadata says refreshed July 2026, while individual geometry dates can be much older.
Download date is not a claim that every road/shoreline was surveyed recently.

Roads use the coarse 1:1,000,000-scale layer. Water polygons are >=2 km2 and may include
seasonal waterbody/playa outlines. Rivers are named Truckee/Carson/Walker StreamRiver
features, with artificial flow-network paths omitted. Runways are actual source polygon
outlines. Geometry is simplified at 100 m in a metric spherical-AEQD projection with
per-feature topology preservation. One invalid source water geometry (445563) was
repaired with Shapely make_valid and recorded in metadata. This is a general reference
basemap, not navigation/survey data; local streets, terrain, filled water and place
labels are not part of this minimal pack.
